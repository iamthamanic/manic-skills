# Halteverbot123 CI Style Guide for Presentations

Use these rules whenever creating or handing off a Halteverbot123 / browo GmbH presentation, partner deck, sales deck, proposal, report, or Gamma/Canva rendering. If another explicit brand system is supplied by the user, ask before overriding this default.

## Brand role

The presentation must feel like a Halteverbot123 business asset, not a generic Gamma/Canva theme.

- Use the brand to signal reliability, clarity, operational competence, and fast implementation.
- Prefer structured business visuals over decorative art.
- Do not use generic startup gradients, purple SaaS palettes, stock AI people, or unrelated abstract imagery.

## Core colors

Use only the corporate colors and neutral support tones.

Primary palette:
- Dark navy: `#1D2E52` (fallback `#192643`) — headlines, dark bands, navigation bars, emphasized text.
- Brand light blue: `#4EB2E5` with interface-support blues `#62A6DA` / `#6299D8` — info highlights, maps, subtle accents, secondary emphasis.
- Coral CTA: `#EC7C69` for standard CTAs and benefit icons; use stronger emphasis orange-red `#E7614C` sparingly for high-attention buttons or numeric badges.
- Warm off-white: `#F9F8F9` / `#F7F6F6` — primary canvas background.
- Quiet card fill: `#FDFBFB` / `#F4EEEE` — soft cards, benefit chips, panels.
- Thin neutral line / text support: `#D8DADF`, `#9EA3AE`, `#5B5D62`.

Accent discipline:
- Avoid unapproved accent colors. In particular, avoid Gamma default purple/indigo accents.
- Do not introduce unrelated greens, violets, neon gradients, or oversaturated reds.
- The deck should feel like the approved screenshots: light editorial canvas, dark navy text, coral CTA system, restrained blue data/brand accents.

## Logo and signature V

- Preserve the logo colors: light blue wordmark, red V, red 123.
- Do not recolor the V or the 123.
- Do not remove the 123.
- Do not distort, crop, rotate, or rebuild the logo.
- Keep clear space around the logo. Do not crowd it with text, cards, icons, or charts.
- Use the red V as a signature element for section starts, titles, decision moments, and success/completion cues.
- Large V background elements are allowed when they support the message and do not reduce readability.

## Typography

- Headline font: Arboria Bold when available.
- Body font: Open Sans Regular when available.
- Widget / secondary UI text: Roboto Medium when available.
- If the renderer cannot use these fonts, instruct it to use a close modern geometric sans for headlines and a highly legible humanist sans for body text.
- Avoid cramped text, tiny captions, and dense paragraphs.

## Approved presentation theme blueprint

The default deck theme must visually match the approved Halteverbot123 screens shared by the user. Treat these screens as the canonical taste reference.

Theme characteristics:
- Bright, premium, spacious B2B interface look.
- Background is warm off-white, not pure white.
- Cards are large, softly rounded, and lightly tinted rather than stark boxed modules.
- Headline color is dark navy.
- CTAs, arrows, chips, step numbers, and the V marker use coral.
- Light blue appears as a restrained brand/data accent, not as the dominant theme wash.
- Navigation bars or section bands may use dark navy blocks with coral CTA buttons.
- Borders are thin and soft; shadows are minimal to none.
- Layouts should feel like premium product marketing / enterprise landing pages, not generic slide templates.

## Layout style

The preferred visual system is clean B2B bento/editorial design:

- Strong single takeaway per slide.
- Big headline, few supporting points.
- Large KPI tiles, bento blocks, process flows, dashboard mockups, timeline strips, and annotated journeys.
- Use asymmetric split layouts like the approved screenshots: headline/content on one side, a large form, flow, dashboard, or proof block on the other.
- Use dark blue section bands or split layouts selectively; keep most slides bright and easy to read.
- Use coral only to guide attention, not as a full decorative wash.
- Use light blue for informational highlighting, maps, and UI surfaces.
- Use generous spacing and large margins. The design should feel calm, productized, and operationally credible.
- Avoid repetitive equal-sized cards across the whole deck.

## Visual assets

- For external partner decks, avoid AI-generated realistic photos unless the user explicitly approves them.
- Prefer diagrams, vector/pictographic icons, UI mockups, maps, KPI tiles, flows, and real brand/company assets supplied by the user.
- If real photos are needed, use user-supplied or approved company photography only; do not create synthetic people, offices, vehicles, customers, or teams.
- Do not invent partner logos, certification badges, customer logos, or screenshots.
- When showing a portal/dashboard that is not live yet, label it clearly as `Beispielhafte Darstellung`, `Konzept`, or `Demo-Werte`.

## Copy tone

- Direct, concrete, commercially useful.
- Avoid hype words such as seamless, game-changing, revolutionary, innovative, best-in-class unless evidence supports them.
- Prefer claims that answer buyer objections: revenue, effort, risk, trust, implementation, tracking, payout, user benefit.
- Do not over-explain Halteverbotszonen to a business partner; explain only what helps them decide.

## Default Gamma / Canva handoff instructions

When handing to Gamma or Canva, include:

- Use the approved Halteverbot123 screenshot theme, not a generic blue corporate theme.
- Base colors: dark navy `#1D2E52` / `#192643`, brand blue `#4EB2E5` with support blues `#62A6DA` / `#6299D8`, coral CTA `#EC7C69` with stronger emphasis `#E7614C`, warm off-white backgrounds `#F9F8F9` / `#F7F6F6`, quiet card fills `#FDFBFB` / `#F4EEEE`.
- Avoid the default Gamma purple/indigo palette and avoid dark-heavy theme fills.
- Prefer a bright premium layout with large rounded cards, editorial spacing, dark navy headings, and coral CTA elements.
- Use bento layouts, KPI tiles, process flows, and dashboard-style mockups selectively.
- Use no AI-generated realistic images; use diagrams, icons, graphic shapes, and approved brand assets only.
- Include a small source note only on evidence-heavy slides; avoid turning slides into footnote pages.
- Preserve slide titles and slide boundaries.
- If the renderer supports native theme choice, prefer the lightest professional theme that can be recolored cleanly; then force the above palette and taste direction.
