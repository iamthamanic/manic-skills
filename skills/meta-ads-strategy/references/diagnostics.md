# Meta diagnostics

Diagnose from the funnel downward.

## No delivery / very low spend

Check policy/review status, schedule, budget/bid constraints, audience size, conversion setup, account limits, and competing ad sets.

## High CPM

Possible causes include narrow audience, expensive auction, low predicted quality/relevance, seasonality, or placement/geography constraints. Do not assume creative is the only cause.

## Good CTR, weak conversion rate

Inspect message match, landing-page speed, offer, form/checkout friction, price, trust, and tracking before changing targeting.

## Weak CTR and weak conversions

Prioritize creative/message-market fit before micro-targeting changes.

## Retargeting frequency rising

Compare reach growth, conversions, CPA, and creative age. If frequency rises while incremental conversions flatten, refresh creative, reduce budget, shorten/reshape the audience, or merge stages.

## Good platform ROAS, weak business outcome

Reconcile attribution windows, duplicate conversions, CRM/order totals, refunds, offline sales, and channel overlap.

## Scaling

Scale only after the campaign has shown repeatable economics across a meaningful evaluation window. Increase spend in controlled steps and define a rollback threshold before changing budget.
