# Socratic Questions

Use 3–5 per round. Always prefix with **why you ask**.

## Architecture & codebase

- Why should this live in `<layer>` and not `<other layer>`?
- Why add a new module instead of extending `<existing file>`?
- Why now — does AGENTS.md prioritize this over deferred work?
- Why client-side only — does anything need server authority?

## Product & UX

- Why will the user notice this — what problem does it solve in one session?
- Why this interaction model vs. simpler (click vs. drag vs. automatic)?
- Why this visual weight — core loop or decorative?

## Technical depth

- Why this library — what's wrong with what we already have?
- Why real-time vs. pre-rendered / batch?
- Why 3D/WebGL — what fails with CSS/Canvas?

## Scope

- Why include X in v1 — can it ship without it?
- Why shared/global vs. feature-local?

## Security & data

- Why trust client input here — who validates?
- Why store this — retention and privacy implications?

## Anti-patterns

- Don't ask questions you can answer from the codebase
- Don't ask more than 5 at once
- Don't ask "what do you want?" when user already stated a rough idea — refine instead
