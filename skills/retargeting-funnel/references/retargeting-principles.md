# Retargeting principles

## Design principles

1. Recency is useful only when intent decays with time.
2. Behavioral depth usually predicts intent better than a generic pageview.
3. Audience overlap creates internal competition and makes reporting harder to interpret.
4. More segments are not automatically more precise; they often create underpowered ad sets.
5. Retargeting budget is constrained by audience capacity, not by how much the advertiser wants to spend.
6. Creative should advance the decision, not merely repeat the prospecting message.
7. Suppression logic is part of campaign architecture, not an afterthought.
8. Platform attribution is one measurement view, not a substitute for first-party business outcomes.

## Meta-specific current evidence to verify before execution

Meta documentation available in 2026 states that narrower audiences can increase costs and creative fatigue, and that Advantage+ audience can expand beyond selected audience suggestions depending on campaign setup. Meta also documents a manual/original-audience path in some campaign flows when tighter audience control is required. The exact controls can vary by objective, account, region, and product rollout, so inspect the current Ads Manager UI before giving click-by-click instructions.

## Privacy and data quality

For customer-list audiences, use legitimately obtained first-party data and follow the platform's current custom-audience terms and applicable privacy requirements. Do not recommend uploading scraped, purchased, or otherwise unauthorized personal data.
